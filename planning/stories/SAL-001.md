# SAL-001: Confirm business model and first-country eligibility

Epic: EPIC-01. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Identify a legally reviewed territory for an advertising-only paid pilot.

## Scope

Confirm business model and first-country eligibility. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Review entity domicile and exact ad/contact flows with local counsel.
- Evaluate Belgium first as a proposal; document Dutch and Swiss expansion questions including local permits, ages, advertising restrictions and operator classification.
- Record self-advertising, fixed fees, exploitation/reporting obligations and review dates.

## Data and API

Country matrix: territory/locality, legal source/effective date, reviewer, minimum age, evidence requirements and enablement decision.

## Failure and Security

Unknown or adverse findings keep the territory disabled; privileged advice stays outside Git.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-001-AC01: Reviewed memo covers the exact entity and advertiser subscription/external-contact flows.
- SAL-001-AC02: Each candidate has an explicit approved, rejected or unresolved assessment.
- SAL-001-AC03: Pilot conditions include local age/permit, content and reporting requirements.
- SAL-001-AC04: Named decision maker records first-territory go/no-go and review date.
