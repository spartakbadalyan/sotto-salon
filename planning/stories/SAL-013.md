# SAL-013: Build privately owned profile drafts and revisions

Epic: EPIC-04. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Let advertisers control one profile without exposing private identity.

## Scope

Build privately owned profile drafts and revisions. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Allow pseudonym, biography, city/region, languages and opted-in external contact; no exact home/live location.
- Implement one active profile per verified advertiser, immutable revisions and consent records.
- Provide immediate unpublish/consent withdrawal; distinguish visibility from billing and review sensitive edits.

## Data and API

Profile/ProfileRevision with owner, policy version, allowed fields and optimistic-concurrency token.

## Failure and Security

Sanitize rendering and contact schemes; protect drafts/previews from unauthorized access and caches.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-013-AC01: Owner can save/preview/submit; another account cannot access drafts.
- SAL-013-AC02: Concurrent edits cannot overwrite submitted revisions silently.
- SAL-013-AC03: Public preview excludes legal identity, birth date and KYC data.
- SAL-013-AC04: Withdrawal/unpublish denies origin immediately and schedules full invalidation.
