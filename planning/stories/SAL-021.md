# SAL-021: Implement human appeals and dispute escalation

Epic: EPIC-06. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Provide meaningful review of restrictions and notice outcomes.

## Scope

Implement human appeals and dispute escalation. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Free internal complaints for covered decisions with at least six months to submit; qualified different reviewer and no solely automated outcome.
- Expose decision history and applicable certified out-of-court redress information.
- Use proportionate misuse warnings; reversal restores only if current publication conditions hold.

## Data and API

Appeal: decision, eligible party, deadline, reviewer, outcome and private notifications.

## Failure and Security

Overturning a decision never bypasses expired billing, consent withdrawal or another restriction.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-021-AC01: Eligible parties can submit throughout the minimum complaint window.
- SAL-021-AC02: Original reviewer cannot decide their own appeal.
- SAL-021-AC03: Every appeal has human reasoning and applicable further-redress information.
- SAL-021-AC04: Reversal restores only eligible revisions, never withdrawn content.
