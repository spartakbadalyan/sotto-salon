Epic: EPIC-02. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Build traceable artifacts and isolated staging.

## Scope

Create CI and isolated deployment foundation. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Automate lint/types/tests, immutable images and dependency/secret checks.

## Failure and Security

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-008-AC02: Staging uses traceable images and private database/storage.
- SAL-008-AC03: TLS and least-privilege access are verified in approved staging.
- SAL-008-AC04: Production release is separately gated from normal CI.
