# SAL-002: Establish compliance, privacy and commercial policies

Epic: EPIC-01. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Convert applicable duties into owned controls before sensitive-data processing.

## Scope

Establish compliance, privacy and commercial policies. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Map DSA classification, Articles 15/19/29 exemptions, Articles 30-32 scope, representative and regulator; no blanket exemption from absent checkout.
- Document purpose-level GDPR bases, Article 9 conditions, DPIA, biometrics, transfers, retention and DPO assessment.
- Review renewals, cancellation/refunds, tax/VAT, business-user status, Swiss law, accessibility and reporting obligations.
- Resolve ad beneficiary/payer disclosure against pseudonymity and visitor-age requirements.

## Data and API

Versioned obligations record applicability, source, owner, control/story, evidence, review trigger and deadline.

## Failure and Security

Unresolved lawful processing or public-disclosure design blocks the affected live flow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-002-AC01: DSA matrix distinguishes hosting duties, size exemptions and conditional marketplace rules.
- SAL-002-AC02: DPIA covers profile, contact, identity, notices, billing, logs and providers.
- SAL-002-AC03: Subscription policy defines price/tax, renewal, withdrawal/cancellation, suspension and refunds.
- SAL-002-AC04: Each unresolved topic has an owner and launch impact; public-identity policy is resolved before publication.
