# SAL-028: Complete localized accessible advertiser and visitor journeys

Epic: EPIC-07. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Make the complete pilot understandable and usable in approved languages.

## Scope

Complete localized accessible advertiser and visitor journeys. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Localize onboarding, verification, directory, billing/cancel, notices/appeals and legal copy; proposed Belgian languages are French/Dutch with English support.
- Test keyboard, screen reader, errors and mobile; professionally review legal translations.
- Publish operator/contact, policies, versioned terms and ranking explanation; no misleading safety guarantee.

## Data and API

Locale catalogue covers date/currency/fallback and policy versions; decisions/purchases bind displayed terms.

## Failure and Security

Missing critical translations and inaccessible reporting block launch; contact sharing stays deliberate.

- SAL-028-AC04: Required legal/contact/help pages work before sign-in and age checks.
