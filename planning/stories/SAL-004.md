# SAL-004: Obtain subscription acquiring feasibility and economics

Epic: EPIC-01. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Establish an approved route for this exact advertising subscription.

## Scope

Obtain subscription acquiring feasibility and economics. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Prepare truthful underwriting dossier for CCBill and alternatives; explicitly describe ads for in-person services.
- Record acquiring approval/category, merchant role, recurring/SCA support, currencies, bank acceptance, reserves, termination and webhook/reconciliation capabilities.
- Model quoted processing, verification, moderation, disputes/refunds, tax and cash reserves; no invented rates.

## Data and API

Contract covers checkout, subscription lookup/cancel, refund and signed events; excludes payouts and bookings.

## Failure and Security

No merchant-category disguise or prohibited-provider workaround. No accepted acquirer means paid launch blocked.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-004-AC01: Underwriting outcome explicitly covers fixed monthly advertiser fees and proposed content.
- SAL-004-AC02: Economics use quotes or clearly marked unknowns, including reserves.
- SAL-004-AC03: Contract supports renewal, cancellation, refunds and authoritative reconciliation.
- SAL-004-AC04: ARC-004 contains selected-provider evidence or an explicit no-go condition.
