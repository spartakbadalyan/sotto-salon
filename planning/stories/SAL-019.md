# SAL-019: Implement renewal, cancellation and entitlement reconciliation

Epic: EPIC-05. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Align advertising rights with the complete subscription lifecycle.

## Scope

Implement renewal, cancellation and entitlement reconciliation. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Model active, past_due, paid-through expiry, cancel-at-period-end, refund and dispute states.
- Reconcile missing/out-of-order events; prevent duplicate subscriptions and double charges.
- Make cancellation easy/confirmed with receipts and reviewed suspension/refund rules; stop renewals for ineligible accounts as policy requires.
- KYC, consent and moderation always override payment.

## Data and API

Subscription and Entitlement are separate projections; cancel/refund operations are idempotent and auditable.

## Failure and Security

Provider cancel failure stays pending with alert, never falsely confirmed; stale events cannot reactivate restrictions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-019-AC01: Renewal extends rights once; failed/expired payment removes rights at reviewed deadline.
- SAL-019-AC02: Cancellation stops future renewal and applies disclosed remaining-period policy.
- SAL-019-AC03: Late success cannot restore suspended/withdrawn/invalid-KYC profiles.
- SAL-019-AC04: Refund/dispute/reconciliation matrix converges without duplicate charges or hidden renewals.
