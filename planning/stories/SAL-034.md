# SAL-034: Implement visitor consent and ePrivacy controls

Epic: EPIC-03. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Obtain and honour lawful visitor consent for non-essential storage without building tracking profiles.

## Scope

Implement visitor consent and ePrivacy controls. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Set only strictly necessary cookies/storage before consent; gate all non-essential storage behind explicit opt-in with equal accept/reject prominence.
- Record versioned consent choices, allow withdrawal at any time, and re-prompt when the consent version changes.
- Keep consent independent of advertiser identity and visitor age assurance; do not use consent state to build behavioural or sensitive-trait profiles.
- Localize the consent notice and policy links per pilot language; keep pre-consent pages (help, reporting, legal) fully functional.

## Data and API

A minimal ConsentChoice record stores category decisions, policy version, timestamp and a bounded opaque reference; no cross-site identifiers or profiles are created.

## Failure and Security

Declining or ignoring the notice leaves only strictly necessary storage active; a consent-service failure defaults to the most privacy-protective state and never blocks reporting or help.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-034-AC01: No non-essential cookie or storage is written before an explicit opt-in.
- SAL-034-AC02: Reject is as easy as accept, and withdrawal removes previously permitted non-essential storage.
- SAL-034-AC03: A changed consent version re-prompts and supersedes the prior recorded choice.
- SAL-034-AC04: Help, reporting and legal pages work with consent declined or the consent service unavailable.
