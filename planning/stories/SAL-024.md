# SAL-024: Implement privacy rights and retention enforcement

Epic: EPIC-07. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Fulfil rights and minimize data lifetime across application and providers.

## Scope

Implement privacy rights and retention enforcement. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Implement access/export, correction, withdrawal, deletion and scoped legal holds with proportionate authentication.
- Separate retained billing records from public removal; track provider deletion and closure/cancellation.
- Version retention for failed KYC, originals, evidence, logs and backups; maintain restore suppression ledger.

## Data and API

DeletionRequest/RetentionPolicy track scope, deadline, provider status and hold basis; exports are private and short-lived.

## Failure and Security

Do not claim full deletion while justified retention/backups remain; no unnecessary identity checks for rights requests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-024-AC01: Closure hides content and invokes reviewed cancellation policy.
- SAL-024-AC02: Export excludes other users and protected reporter identities.
- SAL-024-AC03: Each retention class expires and downstream failures are retried.
- SAL-024-AC04: Hold/deletion evidence distinguishes immediate public removal from backup expiry.
