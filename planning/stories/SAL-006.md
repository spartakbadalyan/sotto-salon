# SAL-006: Define content rules and staffed moderation operations

Epic: EPIC-01. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Create enforceable rules backed by trained review and urgent response.

## Scope

Define content rules and staffed moderation operations. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Separate illegal content from stricter platform/provider rules; cover minors/minor-coded themes, coercion, trafficking, nonconsensual media, impersonation, doxxing and links.
- Define individual self-ads, non-explicit still photos, consent evidence and private review where public faces are obscured.
- Evaluate eligible text/OCR/image/hash and human services; define pilot-language coverage, false-positive review and lawful test cases.
- Set on-call rota, training/wellbeing, reporting deadlines and capacity limits.

## Data and API

Taxonomy includes rule ID, jurisdiction, severity, action, review path and evidence-handling policy.

## Failure and Security

Filters cannot guarantee legality; KYC cannot establish free consent. Missing urgent coverage blocks launch.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-006-AC01: Every prohibited category has examples, actions and appeal/escalation guidance.
- SAL-006-AC02: Every pilot language has trained coverage and urgent on-call staffing.
- SAL-006-AC03: Lawful benchmark measures misses/false positives per category.
- SAL-006-AC04: Legal/provider deadlines and internal service targets are distinguished and approved.
