# SAL-017: Define plans and transparent subscription purchase terms

Epic: EPIC-05. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Offer a clearly priced monthly advertising subscription.

## Scope

Define plans and transparent subscription purchase terms. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- One fixed monthly plan per approved currency with versioned tax presentation.
- Show total, renewal interval, cancellation/refunds and seller identity before affirmative purchase consent.
- First checkout requires KYC and content approval; preserve terms version and reject browser price manipulation.

## Data and API

PlanVersion/PurchaseConsent: integer minor units, currency, interval, tax policy and accepted terms.

## Failure and Security

No floating-point money, hidden renewals or default opt-ins; price changes follow approved notice/consent policy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-017-AC01: Purchase summary matches server amount, tax, currency and renewal.
- SAL-017-AC02: Manipulated amounts and inactive plan IDs are rejected.
- SAL-017-AC03: Consent identifies exact price/terms accepted.
- SAL-017-AC04: Unverified or unapproved advertisers cannot start first checkout.
