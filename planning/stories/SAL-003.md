# SAL-003: Evaluate advertiser identity and visitor age providers

Epic: EPIC-01. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Choose a privacy-conscious advertiser identity and visitor age design.

## Scope

Evaluate advertiser identity and visitor age providers. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Evaluate Yoti with exact-model acceptance, document-country coverage, liveness and accessible alternatives.
- Separate advertiser identity from minimal visitor age assertions; facial age estimation alone is insufficient KYC.
- Review account binding, fraud appeals, expiration/revocation, biometrics, retention/deletion, subprocessors, non-training terms and cost.

## Data and API

Adapter specifies session creation, signed results, provider reference, threshold, freshness, failure and revocation; documents remain provider-hosted by default.

## Failure and Security

Marketing is not contractual approval. Any required local identity retention needs a separately restricted design.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-003-AC01: Scored evaluation covers business acceptance, documents, privacy, fraud and cost.
- SAL-003-AC02: Sandbox proves success, failure, timeout and authenticated callback handling.
- SAL-003-AC03: Advertiser and visitor outputs contain only purpose-required fields.
- SAL-003-AC04: Written acceptance/data terms are recorded or the live-provider decision remains open.
