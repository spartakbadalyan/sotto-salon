# SAL-011: Enforce jurisdiction and local eligibility policies

Epic: EPIC-03. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Enable only reviewed territories under their own rules.

## Scope

Enforce jurisdiction and local eligibility policies. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Implement disabled-by-default country/locality policy, ages, required permit evidence, content rules and approval expiry.
- Separate service location/evidence from visitor policy; IP does not prove residency/eligibility.
- Audit policy changes and implement immediate country shutdown without redeployment.

## Data and API

Versioned CountryPolicy carries enabled localities, approval evidence and effective/review dates.

## Failure and Security

Unknown/expired policies deny new publication and trigger reviewed existing-listing suppression; document geolocation limits.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-011-AC01: Unknown/disabled territories cannot publish via UI or API.
- SAL-011-AC02: Local thresholds/evidence override global defaults.
- SAL-011-AC03: Service-location changes force eligibility and content review.
- SAL-011-AC04: Country shutdown removes listing, contact and media access within 60 seconds.
