# SAL-009: Implement advertiser accounts and staff authorization

Epic: EPIC-02. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Protect advertiser ownership and privileged operations.

## Scope

Implement advertiser accounts and staff authorization. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Implement verified registration, sessions, logout/recovery, rate limits and advertiser MFA option; require staff MFA.
- Separate moderator, appeals, billing support and security permissions.
- Reauthenticate security/contact changes; audit role grants and use safe account recovery.

## Data and API

Account, Session and RoleGrant models; minimal current-user endpoint.

## Failure and Security

Prevent enumeration, CSRF, session fixation and cross-account access; no ID attachments in support email.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-009-AC01: Advertiser A cannot read or mutate advertiser B private records.
- SAL-009-AC02: Staff without MFA cannot access privileged endpoints.
- SAL-009-AC03: Recovery/logout/revocation invalidate sessions within 60 seconds.
- SAL-009-AC04: CSRF, replay, enumeration and brute-force cases fail safely without credential leakage.
