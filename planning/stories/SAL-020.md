# SAL-020: Implement notice intake and reasoned moderation decisions

Epic: EPIC-06. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Offer traceable reporting without requiring an account.

## Scope

Implement notice intake and reasoned moderation decisions. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Accessible localized form collects content location, explanation, good-faith declaration and reviewed contact requirements/anonymous exceptions.
- Acknowledge, triage and communicate action/no-action with reasons and redress; do not solicit illegal imagery.
- Validate trusted-flagger status where applicable; priority is not automatic removal; use proportionate rate limits.

## Data and API

Notice: URL/revision, category, minimal reporter details, timestamps, decision and safe tracking token. Reasons include rule/legal basis and automation used.

## Failure and Security

Keep reporter details out of advertiser notifications/public outputs; duplicates must not erase legitimate reports.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-020-AC01: Unauthenticated person reports a precise listing and receives safe acknowledgement.
- SAL-020-AC02: Urgent reports immediately enter escalation; ordinary cases retain triage deadlines.
- SAL-020-AC03: Restrictions and no-action outcomes have reasons and appropriate notifications.
- SAL-020-AC04: Reporter details/evidence are absent from public reasons and advertiser responses.
