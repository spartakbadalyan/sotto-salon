# SAL-010: Integrate advertiser KYC and consent-aware onboarding

Epic: EPIC-03. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Bind a verified eligible adult to the advertiser account.

## Scope

Integrate advertiser KYC and consent-aware onboarding. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Use hosted document/liveness capture, minimal assertions and country age/freshness rules.
- Authenticate/deduplicate callbacks and reconcile missing events; no raw-document logs.
- Provide retry/manual review and re-verification after security-sensitive changes; never label KYC as proof of safety/consent.

## Data and API

VerificationAttempt: account/provider binding, result, threshold, timestamps, expiry and policy version.

## Failure and Security

Failed, unknown, underage, expired or revoked checks never grant publication.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-010-AC01: Eligible verified result binds only to the intended account.
- SAL-010-AC02: Forged, replayed and wrong-account callbacks cannot grant status.
- SAL-010-AC03: Expiry/revocation suppresses publication and exposes reviewed recovery.
- SAL-010-AC04: Raw IDs/biometric templates never appear in public API, logs or ordinary staff views.
