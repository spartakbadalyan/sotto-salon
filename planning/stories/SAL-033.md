# SAL-033: Deliver transactional notifications without leaking sensitive data

Epic: EPIC-02. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Deliver required account, billing and moderation messages reliably without exposing protected data.

## Scope

Deliver transactional notifications without leaking sensitive data. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Provide a single outbound channel for verification, receipts, renewal/cancellation, notice acknowledgements and appeal outcomes; drive it from the transactional outbox.
- Never place identity documents, KYC assertions, reporter identities, ad text, contact details or card data in message bodies, subjects or logs; link to authenticated in-app views instead.
- Deduplicate and retry idempotently on provider failure; record delivery status and honour suppression/bounce handling.
- Localize templates per pilot language and keep an approved sender/domain configuration separate from marketing mail.

## Data and API

Notification and DeliveryAttempt records bind an event, template version, recipient reference and status; payloads store references, not sensitive content. The provider adapter uses signed configuration and no card or identity fields.

## Failure and Security

Provider outage retries within bounds and alerts staff rather than dropping legally required acknowledgements; a failed send is visible and replayable, never silently lost. Synthetic addresses only outside approved production.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-033-AC01: A triggering event produces exactly one delivered message with an auditable delivery status.
- SAL-033-AC02: Message bodies, subjects and logs contain no identity documents, reporter identities, ad text, contacts or card data.
- SAL-033-AC03: Provider failure retries idempotently without duplicate sends and raises an alert past the bound.
- SAL-033-AC04: Reporter and advertiser notifications never disclose the other party's protected details.
