# SAL-015: Implement moderation filters and human review console

Epic: EPIC-04. Tracking: `planning/roadmap.yaml`.

## Outcome

Require explainable human approval of each submitted revision.

## Scope

Implement moderation filters and human review console. Follow `docs/product/mvp.md` and the roadmap dependencies. No service payments, commissions or booking functionality.

## Requirements

- Combine normalization, multilingual rules, OCR/image signals and lawful hash access where available; version signals and route uncertainty.
- Human pre-publication review covers initial profiles and material edits; high-risk queue has priority.
- Record reason/rule, reviewer and exact revision; minimize KYC access and prohibit own-case approval.

## Data and API

ModerationCase/Decision bind exact profile/assets, policy, signal summary, human reviewer and action.

## Failure and Security

Scanner outage stays pending; low risk score never auto-publishes. Urgent harm enters escalation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- SAL-015-AC01: Each publish candidate has human approval for exact content/assets.
- SAL-015-AC02: Obfuscated text/OCR and multilingual cases route per policy benchmark.
- SAL-015-AC03: Late approval of old revision cannot approve changed content.
- SAL-015-AC04: Reviewers cannot access raw KYC by default or approve own profile.
